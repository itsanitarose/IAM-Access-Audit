# IAM: The Access Audit

**Scenario:** Northwind Health | [IAM Case Studies Series](https://github.com/itsanitarose/IAM-Case-Studies) ↗  
**5 October 2026 · Fictional healthcare organisation**

## 🎫 The Ticket

**Manager's Request:**

> "We've never properly reconciled the patient admin portal against HR. Here are both exports. Tell me what's wrong, what to fix first, and how we stop it happening again."

**Scope:** Reconcile HR employment records against patient administration portal accounts to identify inappropriate access and prioritise security risks.

**Expected Deliverables:**

1. **Findings:** Identify account-level issues, assess risks, assign severity ratings, and recommend corrective actions.
2. **Priority:** Identify the single most urgent finding and justify why it requires immediate attention.
3. **Prevention:** Recommend a control or process improvement for each type of issue identified.
4. **Management Summary:** Present the key findings and recommendations to the IT manager in no more than 100 words.
