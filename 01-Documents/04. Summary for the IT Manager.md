# 04. Summary for the IT Manager


## Summary for the IT Manager

**Immediate priorities:** Confirm whether Tom Reyes’s contract was extended. If not, disable his Admin access and investigate the login. Investigate Sarah Kim’s login after leaving, and review the shared support login and `testadmin`’s ownership.

**Business exposure:** These accounts could allow unauthorised access to patient data, though the records do not prove a breach.

**Root causes:** The findings suggest gaps in removing access when people leave, recording account ownership, and managing shared logins.

**Prevention:** Link account deactivation to HR leaving dates, assign account owners, replace shared logins with individual accounts, and review access regularly.
